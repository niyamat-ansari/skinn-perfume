Make it last

How to wear it

Four small habits take Silk Rose from a few hours to all-day wear. Follow them in order.

01

Prep the skin

Spray onto clean, moisturised skin. Hydrated skin holds fragrance far longer than dry skin.

02

Find your pulse points

Target wrists, the base of the throat and behind the ears — warmth here lifts the scent through the day.

03

Hold & mist

Keep the bottle 15–20 cm away and spray two to three times into the air and skin, not a soaking cloud.

04

Don't rub it in

Let it dry naturally. Rubbing your wrists together crushes the top notes and shortens the trail.
